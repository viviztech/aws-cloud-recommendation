    VISIBLE ASSUMPTIONS

    A decision aid.
    A traceable estimate.

    Prices are imported or reviewed from provider sources and packaged into a dated snapshot. Catalog coverage is limited to listed products and regions. An unavailable result means the catalog has no eligible configuration; it does not mean the provider has no suitable product.

    Only complete estimates can be recommended as lowest cost. A partial estimate shows the priced components and identifies missing ones. Managed and self-managed databases are different requirements. Bundled resources, billing increments, regional eligibility, and allowances are included where documented in each calculation.

    Audience-based sizing is a heuristic. High availability requires the architecture and failover assumptions described in the report. GPU job estimates use your supplied runtime; GPU-hour prices alone do not measure cost per completed task. Capacity is unverified unless explicitly stated.

    Estimates exclude taxes, support plans, negotiated discounts, free credits, and any service not listed. Verify the final architecture and current prices with the provider before purchasing. Currency conversions show their rate and date in the report.
